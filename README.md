# Worth It? — Day 11

Compare product pack prices using normalized unit prices. Worth It? helps shoppers compare different quantities and compatible units, such as grams with kilograms or millilitres with litres.

## Use it

Open `index.html` or visit the live demo. Add each product's shelf price, pack quantity, and unit. Choose a currency label, then compare the price per kilogram, litre, metre, or item. Copy the summary or download a CSV of the entered products.

## Features

- Compares weight, volume, length, and item-count products in separate groups.
- Converts compatible units such as g/kg, ml/L, cm/m, and each/pair/dozen.
- Highlights the lowest normalized unit price within each group.
- Shows the relative per-unit saving compared with the priciest option.
- Supports a selection of common currency labels and a custom label.
- Copies results or exports the comparison as CSV.
- Responsive interface with no account, uploads, analytics, or external libraries.

## Privacy and limits

Calculations happen in the current browser tab. Product details are not uploaded or saved by the app. Compare the final price you would actually pay; taxes, discounts, shipping, and loyalty points are not calculated automatically. Products from different measurement groups cannot be directly compared.
